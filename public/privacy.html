Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Bounce Ball 3D – Enemy Highway respects your privacy. This policy explains how gameplay data is handled.

Data Collection & Storage

The game stores local progress (coins earned, best scores, owned spheres, mission completions, and settings) directly on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage. No sensitive personal information or financial data is ever collected or sold.

Global Leaderboard

When you submit a score to the Global Top 10 Leaderboard, your chosen player nickname and verified game score are transmitted to our public database to display world rankings.

Contact

For questions or feedback regarding this game, contact us via the game distribution portal.
